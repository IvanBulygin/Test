Castle Ruins

CategoryLevel Design
EngineUnreal Engine

Overview

A medieval castle ruins environment designed for a third-person action game. The level focuses on vertical exploration, hidden pathways, and atmospheric storytelling through environmental details and lighting.

Design Goals

Created a compelling exploration space with clear navigation landmarks, reward-driven discovery paths, and a sense of scale that conveys the former grandeur of the castle. Applied principles of guided exploration — using light, sight lines, and architectural framing to lead the player without explicit markers.
