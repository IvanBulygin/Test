Dodge Ball – Neon Edition

CategoryPrototypes
TypeSolo Project

Overview

A neon-themed multiplayer dodge ball game prototype. Players compete in vibrant arenas, dodging and throwing glowing balls in fast-paced rounds with power-ups and environmental hazards.

Key Features

Features physics-based ball throwing mechanics, neon visual effects with bloom and glow shaders, and a dynamic arena system with destructible elements. The prototype supports local multiplayer with customizable match settings.
