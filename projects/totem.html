TOTEM

RolesTechnical Design, Gameplay Design, Sound Design
EngineUnreal Engine
Team Size5 people
Duration8 months

Overview

Totem is a vibrant 3D adventure platformer set in a world of floating islands and ancient totems. Players navigate through lush, colorful environments, solving puzzles and unlocking new abilities as they uncover the mysteries of a lost civilization.

My Contributions

Technical Design

Architected the core gameplay systems in Unreal Engine, including character controllers, physics-based interactions, and a modular ability system. Optimized performance for smooth rendering of expansive open environments.

Gameplay Design

Designed the progression system, ability unlocks, and level flow. Created gameplay loops that balance exploration, puzzle-solving, and platforming to maintain player engagement throughout the experience.

Sound Design

Developed an adaptive audio system that responds to player altitude, biome, and time of day. Created layered ambient soundscapes and satisfying ability sound effects that reinforce the game's playful tone.
