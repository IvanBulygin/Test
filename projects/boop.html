BOOP

RolesGameplay Design, Technical Design, Sound Design
EngineUnreal Engine
Team Size4 people
Players2-4 players

Overview

BOOP is a chaotic local multiplayer party game set in a steampunk-inspired industrial environment. Players compete in fast-paced mini-games involving physics-based mechanics, strategic positioning, and quick reflexes.

My Contributions

Gameplay Design

Designed the core game modes, scoring systems, and player interaction mechanics. Balanced competitive gameplay to ensure accessible fun for casual players while maintaining depth for skilled play.

Technical Design

Implemented the multiplayer framework, physics-based interaction systems, and game state management. Built a flexible mini-game architecture that allows easy creation of new game modes.

Sound Design

Created energetic sound effects and audio feedback that amplify the chaotic fun of multiplayer matches. Designed dynamic music that escalates with gameplay intensity and responds to key moments.
