Legend of Mothman

CategoryBoard Games
Players3-6 players

Overview

A social card game with a slight case of multiple personalities. Mothman is a social card game where players play as different personalities of one character — the MOTHMAN. Since MOTHMAN suffers from dissociative identity disorder, it cannot decide whether it should help people or make fun of them instead.

Highlights

Easy to play social game with a comedy superhero/villain theme. Features a shared protagonist with conflicting motivations, creating dynamic player interactions. Highly replayable thanks to hidden agendas and the unpredictable personality shifts of MOTHMAN. The different personalities in MOTHMAN's head will have to fight against each other in creative and humorous ways.
