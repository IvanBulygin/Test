Photographer On the Hillside

CategoryLevel Design
EngineUnreal Engine

Overview

An atmospheric outdoor environment designed around a hillside photography theme. The level emphasizes mood, natural lighting, and environmental storytelling — inviting the player to explore a moody, contemplative landscape.

Design Goals

Focused on creating a sense of solitude and discovery through careful terrain sculpting, fog and volumetric lighting, and purposeful prop placement. The environment tells a story of someone who found beauty in isolation, using visual composition techniques borrowed from photography itself.
