F R A G M E N T

RolesNarrative Design, Technical Design, Sound Design
EngineUnity
Team Size4 people
Duration6 months

Overview

Fragment is a dark, atmospheric puzzle-platformer that explores themes of memory and identity. Players navigate through a shattered world, piecing together fragments of a forgotten story while solving environmental puzzles tied to the narrative.

My Contributions

Narrative Design

Designed the branching narrative structure, wrote dialogue and environmental storytelling elements. Created the lore bible and character backstories that inform the game's atmosphere and player progression.

Technical Design

Implemented core gameplay systems including the puzzle mechanics, player movement, and interaction framework. Built modular tools for level designers to create new puzzle configurations efficiently.

Sound Design

Created the ambient soundscape and interactive audio system. Designed audio cues that respond to player actions and narrative triggers, enhancing immersion and guiding players through the experience.
